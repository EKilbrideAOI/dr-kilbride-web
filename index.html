Meet Dr. Kilbride

A career built around orthopedic care, sports and active living.

Dr. Kilbride is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon whose interests include sports medicine, joint reconstruction, outpatient total joint surgery, complex knee injuries and traumatic joint injuries.

He completed medical school at LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, orthopedic residency training at UT Southwestern, and fellowship training in arthroscopy and sports medicine at the Southern California Orthopedic Institute. He has practiced orthopedic surgery in Austin since 2002.

His experience includes serving as a team physician and providing sports-event coverage, as well as participating in orthopedic trauma call in the Austin community.

For injured patients

When you are hurt, getting clear answers matters.

Whether your injury happened at work, on the field, on the road or during everyday life, the first step is understanding what is injured and what treatment options make sense.

  • Bring prior imaging and medical records when available.
  • Tell the office how and when the injury occurred.
  • Bring a current medication and allergy list.
  • Ask questions about both surgical and non-surgical options when appropriate.
